# Forever UI art notes

The spellbook training icon uses the same single-image, 128 × 128 RGBA TGA format as `encyclopedia.tga` and `npc-role-trainer.tga`. It has a soft transparent edge and is drawn upright; the button supplies the dark background and gold border. `training-spellbook.png` is the source-sized preview of the converted asset.

`instance-guide.tga` is a single upright 128 × 128 RGBA image with transparent edges, matching the gold-and-brown `encyclopedia.tga` and `addons.tga` icons. Its button supplies the background and border; `instance-guide.png` is the matching preview.

`training-minimal.tga` replaces the training button artwork with a simplified gold book and arrow; `training-minimal.png` is its 128 × 128 preview. The image is trimmed and centered within the existing transparent canvas, leaving the button to draw its own border.
# Healing-frame role badges

The role icons are 128 × 128 RGBA TGA textures with soft transparency, upright artwork, and the same dark engraved outline as the existing Forever UI icons. The tank shield and damage swords reuse the addon's existing miniature art, recolored blue and red; the green healing cross has a thin gold edge. They are drawn inside 14 × 14 badges on the portrait and carry no frame or click behavior of their own.
