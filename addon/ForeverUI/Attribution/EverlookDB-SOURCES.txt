Quest reference enrichment for previously uncategorised quests

Source: EverlookDB, https://everlookdb.com/data.html
Quest and NPC data snapshots from WoW Forever client build 1.60.1.71037.
Credits and individual upstream licenses: https://everlookdb.com/credits.html
Some upstream records incorporate Efficient Games / EG Link observations,
licensed CC BY 4.0: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/ .
Other source data is attributed on the credits page, including Questie and
CMaNGOS data under GPL v3. Game data and names belong to Blizzard Entertainment.

The importer selects matching quest IDs and names and derives compact factual
fields including zone categories, NPC IDs and names, levels, rewards, reputation,
and chains. It does not copy quest dialogue. Missing or approximate values are
not proof of current availability. The input hashes and transformation are in
data/forever-kb/unknown-quest-discoveries.json and
scripts/import-unknown-quest-discoveries.py in the source workspace.

Additional manually checked records list their own source URLs in
data/forever-kb/unknown-quest-manual-verification.json and in the quest UI.

NPC quest relationships are also derived from this snapshot by
scripts/import-npc-quest-relations.py. The generated NpcQuestRelations.lua
records source hashes and supplies reference-only starts and turn-ins.

Where IDs overlap, levels were also compared to forever-quests 1.3.0:
https://github.com/tyler3497/forever-quests (GPL v3), derived from QuestieDB,
TrinityCore and NorgannonDB. Its input manifest hashes are retained in the
workspace snapshot. This older source covers only some of the missing quests;
disagreements are displayed rather than silently treated as verified.
