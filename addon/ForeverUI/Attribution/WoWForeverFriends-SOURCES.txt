Supplemental item-to-NPC source relations

Derived from the WoW Forever Friends structured item and NPC dataset:
https://www.wowforeverfriends.org/library/sources/
https://www.wowforeverfriends.org/items/items.json
https://www.wowforeverfriends.org/library/data/npcs.json

The dataset is adapted from CMaNGOS Classic-DB contributors and distributed
under GPL version 3. It describes Classic reference data, not verified Forever
availability or drop rates. Only item IDs, NPC IDs and NPC names are used;
quest dialogue, editorial notes and drop percentages are not imported.

Quest objective names, reward-choice flags, suggested group size and chain
IDs are derived from the same GPL-3.0 Classic reference dataset:
https://www.wowforeverfriends.org/quests/quests.json
They are labelled as Classic references and are not proof of live Forever
quest requirements, rewards or availability. Source hashes and the extraction
script are kept in classic-quest-details.json and
scripts/import-forever-friends-quests.py in the source workspace.

NPC quest-start and turn-in IDs are also extracted by
scripts/import-npc-quest-relations.py after matching quest IDs and names and
NPC IDs and names. They remain Classic references and are not evidence of
current server availability. Source hashes are in NpcQuestRelations.lua.

GPL v3 text is included alongside this notice in OneForAll-LICENSE.txt.
The transformation and source hashes are retained in
scripts/import-forever-friends-sources.py and data/forever-kb/
classic-extra-sources.json in the source workspace.
